Version 1.3 · 19 November 2026 Latest

Play the computer.

A bot to play X01 against at fifteen levels, your sets of darts kept track of, a throw at the bull for who goes first, and a Play screen and Settings that are easier on a phone.

Play the computerPlay against the computer in X01: tap Bot on the Play screen and pick its level, from 1 (a beginner averaging about 18) to 15 (averaging about 110 and hitting 80% of its doubles). It throws by itself, and each of its darts is shown landing on the board (you can switch that off under Settings › In a game).
Keep track of your dartsKeep track of your darts: add each set you own to your card under Settings › Players and darts, pick which you're throwing on the Play screen, and see how much each set has been thrown and its stats on their own.
Players and darts in one placePlayers and their darts are now looked after in one place: Settings › Players and darts has a card for each player. Tap it to change their name, add or change their darts, or remove them.
Who throws firstWith two or more playing, Start and Play again now ask who throws first: Closest to bull, where everyone throws one dart at the bull and the nearest starts, or Random.
  • The caller says whose turn it is. With two or more playing, the caller now says whose turn it is at the start of every visit, in every game.
  • Random Targets. Random Targets: hit the target and your next dart is at a new one, so a good visit can take three targets, and each player starts their turn on a new target. Each new target is called out, and a new hard mode splits every single into its inner and outer half.
  • Around the Clock. Around the Clock: the number you need is now called out at the start of every turn and after each hit.
  • Time played. Stats now show how long you've played: Time played adds up every game you've thrown in, for all games or one game type, and the Games tab shows the total time for each.
  • Play in three steps on a phone. On a phone, the Play screen is now three simple steps: Game, Options and Players. Each shows what is chosen at a glance and opens when you tap it, every game says what it is, and Start is always in reach.
  • Settings on a phone. Settings are easier to find your way round on a phone: a short list shows how everything is set, and a tap opens one thing at a time. On and off are now switches, feedback has its own place, and the version is under About.
  • Quit game asks first. Quit game now asks if you're sure, and lets you save your results: leave the box ticked and the darts you threw still count in your stats.
  • Your device's own voice. Scores are now called only in your device's own voices. A device with none, such as a Raspberry Pi, no longer uses the old robotic voice and stays quiet instead.
  • Updates checked as genuine. Updates are now checked before they are opened: the app only installs a new version that is signed as genuine.
  • Build number. Settings now shows the app's build number next to its version, so you can tell exactly which one you have.
  • Windows report pictures. On Windows, reporting a wrongly scored dart now saves its pictures even when your user name has accented or non-English letters in it.

Version 1.2

Truer doubles and trebles.

  • Calibration for wide webcams. Doubles and trebles are scored more accurately: automatic calibration now allows for the way wide webcams bend the picture. Run Auto calibrate again on each camera to get this.

Version 1.1

Updates that find you.

  • New versions offered in the app. The desktop app now tells you when a new version is ready, shows what has changed, and can download it for you.
