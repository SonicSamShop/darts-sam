What's new

Every version of Darts S.A.M and what changed in it, newest first. The site is always the newest; the app on the computer by your board offers each new version when it starts.

Versions: 1.3 · 1.2 · 1.1 · 1.0

Version 1.3 Latest

19 November 2026

A bot to play X01 against at fifteen levels, your sets of darts kept track of, a throw at the bull for who goes first, and a Play screen and Settings that are easier on a phone.

  • Play against the computer in X01: tap Bot on the Play screen and pick its level, from 1 (a beginner averaging about 18) to 15 (averaging about 110 and hitting 80% of its doubles). It throws by itself, and each of its darts is shown landing on the board (you can switch that off under Settings › In a game).
  • Keep track of your darts: add each set you own to your card under Settings › Players and darts, pick which you're throwing on the Play screen, and see how much each set has been thrown and its stats on their own.
  • Players and their darts are now looked after in one place: Settings › Players and darts has a card for each player. Tap it to change their name, add or change their darts, or remove them.
  • With two or more playing, Start and Play again now ask who throws first: Closest to bull, where everyone throws one dart at the bull and the nearest starts, or Random.
  • With two or more playing, the caller now says whose turn it is at the start of every visit, in every game.
  • Random Targets: hit the target and your next dart is at a new one, so a good visit can take three targets, and each player starts their turn on a new target. Each new target is called out, and a new hard mode splits every single into its inner and outer half.
  • Around the Clock: the number you need is now called out at the start of every turn and after each hit.
  • Stats now show how long you've played: Time played adds up every game you've thrown in, for all games or one game type, and the Games tab shows the total time for each.
  • On a phone, the Play screen is now three simple steps: Game, Options and Players. Each shows what is chosen at a glance and opens when you tap it, every game says what it is, and Start is always in reach.
  • Settings are easier to find your way round on a phone: a short list shows how everything is set, and a tap opens one thing at a time. On and off are now switches, feedback has its own place, and the version is under About.
  • Quit game now asks if you're sure, and lets you save your results: leave the box ticked and the darts you threw still count in your stats.
  • Scores are now called only in your device's own voices. A device with none, such as a Raspberry Pi, no longer uses the old robotic voice and stays quiet instead.
  • Updates are now checked before they are opened: the app only installs a new version that is signed as genuine.
  • Settings now shows the app's build number next to its version, so you can tell exactly which one you have.
  • On Windows, reporting a wrongly scored dart now saves its pictures even when your user name has accented or non-English letters in it.

Version 1.2

  • Doubles and trebles are scored more accurately: automatic calibration now allows for the way wide webcams bend the picture. Run Auto calibrate again on each camera to get this.

Version 1.1

  • The desktop app now tells you when a new version is ready, shows what has changed, and can download it for you.
