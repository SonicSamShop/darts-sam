What's new

Every version of Darts S.A.M and what changed in it, newest first. The site is always the newest; the app on the computer by your board offers each new version when it starts.

Version 1.10.1 Latest

27 November 2026

121 Checkout fixes and head-to-head play, plus a configurable delay between turns.

  • 121 Checkout: Highest checkout now records the 3-dart finishing checkout you hit, rather than the starting target score.
  • 121 Checkout with two or more players: head to head on the same target — first to check it out wins the leg, and the most legs wins.
  • Delay between turns (2+ players): after a visit ends, throws for the next player wait 4 seconds before counting to prevent accidental double-counting (change or switch off under Settings › In a game).

Version 1.10

27 November 2026

99 Darts training, deeper X01 stats with trend charts and board dot plots, and TV camera zooming.

  • Stats: X01 visits below 100 are now counted in steps of ten (90+, 80+, 70+, 60+, 50+), in the tiles and in the visit scoring breakdown.
  • New training game: 99 Darts. Pick a number or the bull and throw 33, 66 or 99 darts at it; every dart in it is a hit, with singles, doubles and trebles counted apart, and its own stats.
  • More X01 stats: the 3-dart average day by day, how often scoring darts find the 20, the treble 20 and the 1 or 5, the points of the 1st, 2nd and 3rd dart, checkouts from 2-40, 41-80 and 81-170, your best and weakest double, visits under 30 and 26s.
  • The board on the Stats screen can now show each dart as a dot where it landed, the heat map, or both, and the heat map is sharper, so separate groups of darts stay apart.
  • TV camera in X01: dynamic framing and zooming on the board as you play and finish.
  • Stats: switch the 3-dart average trend chart between time scales and hover or tap points for detailed tooltips.

Version 1.9.1

26 November 2026

A clearer, more prominent voice scoring button in the game header.

  • Voice scoring button is now clearly visible and prominent in the game header, showing its microphone icon and live status (Voice, Listening or Paused).

Version 1.9

26 November 2026

Hands-free voice scoring, automatic desktop updates, player country flags, and sharper camera detection.

  • The desktop app now updates itself: a new version is downloaded, installed and restarted onto by itself as soon as no game is going, with what's new shown. To wait until you next open Darts S.A.M instead, switch off Restart for updates between games under Settings › About.
  • Voice scoring: tap the microphone button on the game screen (or press V) to call your darts or visit totals aloud from the oche — including "treble 20", "tops", "bull", "no score", "bust" and "undo".
  • Player country flags: pick a flag for each player under Settings › Players, darts and boards to display beside their name on the scoreboard, TV view and share cards. The caller now also announces who throws first at the start of every match and new leg or set.
  • More accurate camera dart detection: improved barrel line fitting and tip scoring for cleaner hits across cameras.
  • Pick your look right away on the welcome screen, and watch the computer's darts fly in with a new tungsten steel-tip dart animation.

Version 1.8.4

26 November 2026

Fewer darts on the wire scored in the wrong bed.

  • Darts on a wire are scored more accurately with two or more cameras. When a dart sits right on the edge of a treble, double or number, the cameras' own views of its point now decide which side of the wire it is on.

Version 1.8.3

26 November 2026

How everyone played, at the end of every game.

  • Match stats when a game is over: everyone's 3-dart and first 9 averages, 180s, 140+ and 100+, checkout rate, highest checkout, best leg, trebles, doubles and more, side by side, with each leg's average in X01. Tap Show the board to see the board again. The same summary now heads every game's scoresheet in Stats.

Version 1.8.2

25 November 2026

A cleaner game screen, and a way to check your darts before play moves on.

  • New setting under Settings › In a game: Keep my darts up after a visit. Your three darts stay on screen for up to 15 seconds, counting down, so a wrongly scored dart is easy to spot and put right with Undo before play moves on.
  • A tidier game screen. The three darts of a visit and their total sit together in one box, each labelled, and the last visit shows what it scored in large figures with its darts beside it.
  • End turn (it was Next player) is now the main button. Undo and Miss are outlined, and TV view, Type scores and Quit game have moved to a quiet row of links underneath.
  • Little touches as you play: the bed a dart lands in lights up for a moment, the remaining score counts down to its new figure, a bust flashes red and a finish gold. Devices set to reduce motion don't get them.

Version 1.8.1

25 November 2026

A small change to tournaments.

  • Tournaments: quitting a match now means giving it up. Whoever quits is out, and their opponent goes through with the match won by the full count of legs.

Version 1.8

25 November 2026

Tournaments for the whole household, and scoring with a phone's own camera is far more accurate.

  • Tournaments: a knockout for 3 to 16 players, people and bots, on one board.
  • 121 Checkout now says "Can't check out" when the darts you have left can't finish what's left, instead of "Bust", and those darts still count.
  • Phone camera (Beta): after it finds the board it now says if the board is too dark, too small in the picture or seen too far from the side, and what to change.
  • Phone camera (Beta): far more accurate. It now finds the dart's thin steel point instead of stopping where the barrel starts, lines each picture back up with the board when the phone tips on its stand, and asks "Which bed?" less often.
  • Phone camera (Beta): the "Which bed?" question no longer shows the board through it in the Chalk & Oche and League Scoresheet themes.

Version 1.7.3

23 November 2026

A fix for cameras scoring the wrong numbers after 1.7.2.

  • Fixed: after updating to 1.7.2 a camera could score every dart as the wrong number, with its green 20 in the wrong place. A camera lined up at another picture size is now scaled to the size in use.
  • Find the board now asks you to tap the 20 every time you press it, so a camera whose numbers have come out turned round is put right with one tap.

Version 1.7.2

23 November 2026

A new Cameras screen: every camera side by side, which camera goes where, picture size and standby, and a way to line up a camera that can't see the 20.

  • A camera you switched off can be switched back on while other cameras are running: Add a camera lists it with Use this camera. Before, the app said no new camera was found.
  • The Cameras screen now shows every camera side by side, each with its green lines and an edge that says whether it is ready. Tap a picture to open that camera by itself.
  • Choose which camera goes in each place from the list under its picture, or switch one off there. Handy when a computer numbers its cameras differently after they are plugged in again.
  • Picture size and frames a second can be set on the Cameras screen, and Find the board on all lines up every camera at once.
  • The cameras rest after a while unused (15 minutes to start with; change it on the Cameras screen) and wake when a game starts or you open the Cameras screen.
  • A camera that can't see the 20 can still find the board: pick a number it can see, and tap that instead.

Version 1.7.1

23 November 2026

Three cameras through one USB hub on Windows, and how Darts S.A.M compares, in the app.

  • Settings, About now shows how Darts S.A.M compares with Autodarts and DartCounter: what each costs, camera scoring, games and playing online.
  • Windows: three USB cameras through one USB hub now all show a picture. With 1.7 some computers still showed only one.

Version 1.7

23 November 2026

Several cameras through a USB hub on Windows, more than one board with stats for each, and two new pages on the website.

  • Keep more than one board, and see your stats on each one side by side: Settings › Players, darts and boards lists your boards with the one on the wall, and Stats has a Board picker beside Darts.
  • The website has two new pages: how autoscoring with webcams works and what to buy, and how Darts S.A.M compares with Autodarts and DartCounter. Links shared in chats now show a picture of the app.
  • Windows: more than one USB camera through a USB hub now works. Before, only one of them showed a picture.

Version 1.6

22 November 2026

Achievements, a computer opponent that plays a steadier leg and suggests its own level, each visit's score shown large, and more about your darts and your board.

  • When a visit is over, what it scored (or Bust) takes over the scores, large, for a couple of seconds, so it can be read from the oche when the voice can't be heard. Settings › In a game switches it off.
  • Choosing X01 now shows your own average under the players, and the bot's level is suggested from it: the first level that averages as much as you or more (on 40, the level that averages 44). A level you set yourself stays put, with the suggested one a tap away.
  • Playing the computer, you can now choose how long it waits after your visit before it throws: anything from no wait to 30 seconds, under Settings › In a game. It stays at 8 seconds unless you change it.
  • The computer plays a steadier leg, most of all at the lower levels: it scores nearer its level's average on the way down and goes bust far less once it is on a finish, where before it scored high and then lost its average at the end. Each level still averages what it says and hits its doubles as often. A dart of its that misses the board now always shows beside the board, where a wild one could land out of the picture.
  • Achievements. A first game of each kind, a first 180, a 170 finish, a 501 leg in 15 darts (about sixty in all) are shown on the game screen and called as you earn them, with everything earned listed when the game ends. Stats has a new Achievements tab for each player: the ones held, when and in which game, and the ones still to get. Games you have already played count. Settings › In a game switches the showing off.
  • Your darts can now say more about themselves: under Settings › Players and darts, More details on a set keeps its brand, tungsten, barrel shape, grip, shaft, flight and a note.
  • Settings has a new Your board section: keep your board's brand, model, the year you bought it and a note (when you last turned it, say).
  • Stats now puts your sets of darts side by side: the 3-dart average and checkout rate in X01, and trebles hit, for each set. A figure waits ("not yet") until a set has enough darts behind it to mean something.
  • When a camera scores the dart that earns an achievement, a picture of the board is kept with it, darts and all, and shown when you open the achievement. On the site, and when a phone's own camera is scoring, the app asks first. Settings › In a game switches it off, and a picture is deleted with its game.

Version 1.5

21 November 2026

Six new games, five of them for training, the rules of every game written out, scoring with your phone's camera (Beta), and cameras that line themselves up when the app opens.

  • The cameras now line themselves up each time you open the app: every camera that has been lined up before finds the board again by itself, so a camera that got nudged no longer needs Find the board again pressed on each one. Switch it off on the Cameras screen (Auto-calibrate on start).
  • Every game now has its rules written out: on the Play screen, tap How to play under the game you have chosen to read how it works and what each option changes, without leaving the game menu.
  • Score with your phone's camera (Beta): on the site, open Cameras and press Use this Device Camera. Stand the phone beside the board and it scores each dart by itself, with no computer, and asks which bed when it isn't sure.
  • Killer has two new options: become a killer with a treble instead of a double, and take lives by hitting any part of a player's number (single, double or treble) instead of only their double.
  • Every option of every game now has a ? beside it: tap it to see what the setting you have chosen means. On a computer, hold the pointer over a choice to see what it would mean before you pick it.
  • Killer now needs two or more players, as its rules say. Started with one, it used to end on the first dart.
  • When Start can't be pressed, a line above it now says why: the game needs more players, or the bot has nobody to play.
  • In the League Scoresheet and Soft-tip Arcade themes, the links on the Play screen are no longer drawn as boxes.
  • Playing the computer, it now waits 8 seconds after your visit before it throws, with a countdown, so you can undo a wrongly scored dart first. Tap Throw now to skip the wait.
  • The Play screen now splits the games into Matches and Training, so the practice games are easy to find.
  • Five new training games: Bob's 27 (every double in turn, starting on 27), Shanghai, Bermuda, Segment Training (pick a number or the bull and see how often you hit it) and Random Checkout. Each has its own stats.
  • Around the Clock has a Round the World option: count only doubles, or only trebles.
  • New game: Gotcha, a race to exactly 301 or 501, where landing on another player's score sends them back to zero.

Version 1.4

20 November 2026

A plainer, easier to read look: stats as a list, a new Classic theme in the board's own colours, Chalk & Oche in one hand throughout, and sound that starts off until you want it.

  • Stats are now set out as a list, each figure on a line beside its name, which is easier to read, on a phone most of all.
  • The Classic theme has a new plain look in the board's own colours: cream for what is chosen, green for the button that moves the game on.
  • Cricket's marks are now drawn the way a chalker draws them, a stroke, a cross and a ringed cross, and look the same on every device.
  • The website's pages (the home page, the questions, the downloads and the release notes) have been rewritten to be plainer and easier to read.
  • The Chalk & Oche theme is now written in one hand throughout, and its sevens no longer have a bar through them.
  • Names with letters such as ł, š or ğ are now drawn in each theme's own lettering, instead of borrowing those letters from another.
  • Sound is now off to begin with: switch it on under Settings › In a game. With it on, the caller says each visit's total; a new Call each dart switch has every dart called as well.

Version 1.3

19 November 2026

A bot to play X01 against at fifteen levels, your sets of darts kept track of, a throw at the bull for who goes first, and a Play screen and Settings that are easier on a phone.

  • Play against the computer in X01: tap Bot on the Play screen and pick its level, from 1 (a beginner averaging about 18) to 15 (averaging about 110 and hitting 80% of its doubles). It throws by itself, and each of its darts is shown landing on the board (you can switch that off under Settings › In a game).
  • Keep track of your darts: add each set you own to your card under Settings › Players and darts, pick which you're throwing on the Play screen, and see how much each set has been thrown and its stats on their own.
  • Players and their darts are now looked after in one place: Settings › Players and darts has a card for each player. Tap it to change their name, add or change their darts, or remove them.
  • With two or more playing, Start and Play again now ask who throws first: Closest to bull, where everyone throws one dart at the bull and the nearest starts, or Random.
  • With two or more playing, the caller now says whose turn it is at the start of every visit, in every game.
  • Random Targets: hit the target and your next dart is at a new one, so a good visit can take three targets, and each player starts their turn on a new target. Each new target is called out, and a new hard mode splits every single into its inner and outer half.
  • Around the Clock: the number you need is now called out at the start of every turn and after each hit.
  • Stats now show how long you've played: Time played adds up every game you've thrown in, for all games or one game type, and the Games tab shows the total time for each.
  • On a phone, the Play screen is now three simple steps: Game, Options and Players. Each shows what is chosen at a glance and opens when you tap it, every game says what it is, and Start is always in reach.
  • Settings are easier to find your way round on a phone: a short list shows how everything is set, and a tap opens one thing at a time. On and off are now switches, feedback has its own place, and the version is under About.
  • Quit game now asks if you're sure, and lets you save your results: leave the box ticked and the darts you threw still count in your stats.
  • Scores are now called only in your device's own voices. A device with none, such as a Raspberry Pi, no longer uses the old robotic voice and stays quiet instead.
  • Updates are now checked before they are opened: the app only installs a new version that is signed as genuine.
  • Settings now shows the app's build number next to its version, so you can tell exactly which one you have.
  • On Windows, reporting a wrongly scored dart now saves its pictures even when your user name has accented or non-English letters in it.
