What is stored about you

Darts S.A.M is being tried out by a small group of players. This page says what the trial keeps about you, why, and how to remove it.

What is stored

  • Google's or Apple's number for your account. When you sign in with Google or Apple, they tell this site a number that stands for your account, so the site knows it is you next time. Your email address and picture are not stored.
  • If you sign in by email, a fingerprint of your address. Your address is used to send you the sign-in code and is then not kept. The site keeps a scrambled form of it, which it cannot read back, so it knows it is you next time. The company that delivers the email (Resend) handles your address to deliver it.
  • Your first name, as Google or Apple gives it. It becomes your account's name and your first player. You can rename the player in the app. (By email there is no name: you start as "Player".)
  • Your players, games and stats: every game you save and each dart in it.
  • Problem reports, only when you press Report: what the cameras saw for the last few darts, including the cameras' pictures, and anything you type about what went wrong.
  • When you joined, each time you signed in, and when you last used the site.

A cookie keeps you signed in on this device for up to 90 days. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

To count visits, the pages use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and does not follow you between sites. It records the page you opened, the site you came from, and your browser, device type and country, and these are only ever seen added up.

Why

To save your games and show your stats on any device you sign in on, and to find out how well the automatic scoring works and where it goes wrong, so it can be improved.

For how long

Problem reports are deleted after 60 days. Everything else is kept until you delete your account or the trial ends.

Who sees it

Only the person running the trial. Nothing is sold or passed to anyone else, apart from your address going to the email delivery company when you ask for a sign-in code.

The site shows two totals to everyone: how many darts all its players have thrown between them, and in how many games. Nothing in them says who threw what.

Deleting it

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete my account. That removes your account, your games and stats, and your reports, straight away and for good.

The desktop app, used without an account

The app you download to score with your cameras works by itself, with no account. The first time it is opened it asks whether it may send what it records to Darts S.A.M. If you say no, nothing it records leaves your computer. (Each time it starts, it asks app.dartssam.co.uk whether there is a newer version of the app. That question says only which kind of computer it is: Mac, Windows or Linux.) If you say yes, it sends:

  • Your games and scores: every game and each dart in it. Players are sent as "Player 1", "Player 2": their names are not sent.
  • How the cameras did: how many cameras there are, whether calibrating them worked, when a dart they scored was put right by hand, which kind of computer it is and the app's version.
  • Problem reports, only when you press Report: what the cameras saw for the last few darts, including the cameras' pictures, and anything you type about what went wrong.
  • Feedback you choose to write in Settings → Sharing.

No account, name or email address is asked for or sent. What is sent is kept under a random number made for your copy of the app, for the same reason and as long as above (problem reports for 60 days), and is seen only by the person running the trial. To stop, switch it off in Settings → Sharing in the app. Delete what has been sent, on the same page, removes all of it from Darts S.A.M straight away and for good; your games on your own computer are not touched.
