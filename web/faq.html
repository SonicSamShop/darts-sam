Questions and answers

How to connect your cameras, how the scoring works, what happens to your data and what the project is for.

On this page: How to connect · How it works · Your data · The project

How do I connect?

To keep score by hand you need nothing but this site: sign in and tap the board where each dart landed. To have the darts score themselves, connect the cameras at your board to your account:

  1. Get the app. On the Mac, Windows or Linux PC by your board, download Darts S.A.M, open it and plug in your cameras.
  2. Line up the cameras. The app opens on Cameras. Take the darts out of the board and press Find the board for each camera.
  3. Connect to your account. Press Connect to your account in the app. It shows a 6-letter code: type it on this site under Cameras.

From then on every phone, tablet and TV you sign in on scores by itself.

What do I need?

  • A Mac, a Windows 10 or 11 PC, or a Linux PC (Ubuntu 22.04 or newer) near the board, to plug the cameras into.
  • A camera that sees the whole board: a USB webcam is what most people use. A phone running an IP-webcam app on the same Wi-Fi works too.
  • A phone, tablet or TV to show the scores.

How many cameras do I need?

Two or three, looking at the board from different sides: the tip of the dart is found where their views of it cross. One camera will score, but less surely.

I don't have cameras. Can I still use it?

Yes. Sign in and tap the board where each dart landed, or type in each visit's score. Everything else is the same: the caller, the games and the stats.

How do I sign in?

With the buttons on the home page. There is no form to fill in and no password to remember: the first time you sign in makes your account.

Can I use more than one screen?

Yes. Sign in with the same account on each one. A phone at the oche, a tablet on the wall and the TV all show the same cameras' darts.

How do I put it on my home screen?

On an iPhone or iPad, open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome, tap the menu ⋮, then Add to Home screen. It then opens full screen, like any other app.

How do I get it on the TV?

Open the site on the TV, or on a computer plugged into it, and sign in. In a game, press TV view: the board takes the screen and the scores run along the bottom.

The app will not open the first time.

Mac and Windows both ask before opening an app they have not seen before; on Linux you run install.sh once. The download page says what to do on each.

How does it work?

Darts S.A.M is a scoreboard that runs in your browser. Tell it where each dart landed and it does the adding up, calls the score, says what you need for the finish and keeps your stats. With cameras, the telling is done for you.

How do the cameras score a dart?

Each camera compares its picture from just before the dart landed with the one just after. What changed is the dart. Darts S.A.M follows the line of the dart down to the board, and where the lines from the cameras meet is the tip. It then looks up which bed that is: a treble 20, a double 16, the bull.

This is done by the app on your own computer, by the board.

What does "Find the board" do?

It works out where the board is in each camera's picture, so that a spot in the picture can be turned into a place on the board. Do it once when you set up, and again if a camera is moved or knocked.

What if it scores a dart wrongly?

Press Undo and tap the board where the dart really is. If you have a moment, press Report as well: it saves what the cameras saw for the last few darts, which is what is needed to find out why.

Which games can I play?

Fourteen. For matches: X01 (301, 501 and 701, with legs and sets), Cricket, Killer and Gotcha. For training: Around the Clock, Bob's 27, Shanghai, Bermuda, Segment Training, Random Checkout, 121 Checkout, Count Up, Random Targets and Free Mode. The Play screen keeps the two apart.

What stats does it keep?

For every player: three-dart and first-nine averages, checkout rate, highest checkout, 180s and 140s, legs won, and a heat map of where your darts land. Tap any number to see the legs behind it.

Does it work without the internet?

Scoring by hand does. Once the site is on your home screen it opens and runs with no connection, keeps your games on the device and sends them when you are next online.

Camera scoring through this site needs the computer by the board and the screen you are scoring on to both be online.

How do we use the data?

For two things: to keep your games and stats, so they are there on any device you sign in on, and to find out how well the camera scoring works and where it goes wrong, so it can be improved.

Nothing is sold or passed on, and there are no adverts and no tracking cookies. The full account is on What is stored about you.

What is stored about me?

  • A number that stands for your sign-in, so the site knows it is you next time. Your email address and picture are not stored.
  • Your first name, which becomes your first player. You can change it.
  • Your players, and every game you save with each dart in it.
  • When you joined and when you last used the site.
  • Problem reports and feedback, only when you choose to send them.

Do the camera pictures leave my computer?

The video is worked on by the app on your own computer. What it sends to the site is each dart: its score and where on the board it landed.

Pictures are sent in two cases. When you open Cameras on the site, it shows you what each camera sees: those pictures pass through and are not kept. When you press Report, the cameras' pictures of the last few darts are saved with it, and deleted after 60 days.

Who can see my games?

You, on any device you sign in on, and the person running the trial. Nobody else.

What is the number of darts on the home page?

Every dart thrown by everyone, added together, and the number of games they were thrown in. Two totals: nothing in them says who threw what.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings, then Account, and press Delete my account. Your account, games, stats and reports are removed straight away and for good.

What about the desktop app, used without an account?

The first time it opens, it asks whether it may send what it records to Darts S.A.M. Say no and nothing leaves your computer. Say yes and it sends your games (players as "Player 1", "Player 2", never their names), how the cameras did, and any reports or feedback you choose to send. You can switch it off, and delete what has been sent, in Settings under Sharing.

What is Darts S.A.M for?

Darts S.A.M, your Scoring Assisted Marker, sets out to give any dartboard a scorer that does the job by itself: ordinary cameras pointed at the board, the screens you already own, and no adding up.

It is being tried out now by a small group of players on their own boards. The trial is to find out how well the camera scoring holds up in real rooms, with real lighting and real throws, and to put right what it gets wrong.

Does it cost anything?

The trial is free, and there are no adverts.

How can I help?

Play. When the cameras get a dart wrong, press Report. And tell us what works, what doesn't and what's missing: there is a box for it in Settings under Send feedback.

Who do I ask about something else?

Write to sonicsamshop@gmail.com.
