Autoscore built in

Throw. It scores.

Point cameras at your board and Darts S.A.M sees where each dart lands and scores it for you. The cameras plug into a Mac, a Windows PC or a Linux PC near the board. This is the app that runs on it.

You are on a phone or tablet. Open this page on the computer your cameras are plugged into: dartssam.co.uk/download.html

Three steps

From download to the first dart

1. Open itPlug in your cameras and start Darts S.A.M. Its page opens in your browser.
2. Line up the camerasThe app opens on Cameras. Take the darts out of the board and press Find the board for each camera.
3. Connect to your accountPress Connect to your account there. It shows a 6-letter code: type it on this site under Cameras. Now any phone, tablet or TV you sign in on scores by itself.

The first time you open it

Your computer will ask

On a MacOpen the download and drag Darts S.A.M to Applications. The first time, macOS won't open it: go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. It then asks once whether it may use the cameras.
On WindowsDouble-click DartsSAM.exe. The first time, Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway. Darts S.A.M opens in its own window: close it when you've finished playing.
On LinuxUnpack the download, open the DartsSAM folder in a terminal and type ./install.sh. Start Darts S.A.M from your app menu: a terminal window shows it is running and its page opens in your browser. Close that window when you've finished playing.
No cameras?You don't need this app. Sign in on the site and score by tapping the board.
