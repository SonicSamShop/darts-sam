# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies everywhere the project is discussed: issues, pull requests,
discussions, and anywhere else someone is speaking for it.

In short:

- Be kind and patient. People here are giving their own time, and many are new
  to programming, to cameras or to darts.
- Disagree about the idea, not the person. No insults, no harassment, no
  unwelcome attention, nothing sexual.
- Don't post anyone's private details, or pictures of people who haven't
  agreed to it. Camera pictures in reports should show the board, not the room.
- Accept that the maintainer may edit or remove a comment, close a thread, or
  block someone who keeps breaking this.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here worries you, write to sonicsamshop@gmail.com. Say
what happened and where (a link helps). It will be read by the maintainer, kept
private, and acted on as the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
describe: a private word first, and a ban for repeated or serious harm.
